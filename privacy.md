# Privacy Policy — Tip Breakdown

**Last updated: 2 October 2026**

Tip Breakdown is a tip-out calculator for service workers, published by
Michael Sullivan.

## The short version

Tip Breakdown does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data.
Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no servers, no
accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## What the app stores

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, in the app's
private storage:

- **Your name and settings** — text size, appearance, reporting preset,
  and line names — so the app remembers them between launches.
- **Your roster** — the names and roles of the people you work with.
- **Your locations** — the names of the places you work, if you add any,
  so you can pick one when entering a shift.
- **Your tip log** — a CSV file of the shifts you have chosen to save,
  including the date, location, tip amounts, net sales, and each
  person's payout.
- **Your cash log**, only if you use it — the cash splits you have
  chosen to keep with the **Save** button on the Tip Calculator: the
  date, the total, what was left over, and each person's name and share.
  It is kept apart from the tip log. The app never shares, exports, or
  opens it in another app; you can view it, or delete all of it, in
  Settings → Cash Log.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it.

All of it is included in your phone's own backup — iCloud
on iPhone, Google on Android — if you have that backup turned on. That
backup belongs to your Apple or Google account; the app has no access to
it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create an account or ask for an email address.
- It does not connect to the internet. The app makes no network requests
  of any kind.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising.
- It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or
  microphone.

## Sharing a shift

When you tap **Share Breakdown** or **Send CSV File**, the app hands a
text summary or a CSV file, respectively, to your device's standard share
sheet. You choose what happens next — a messaging
or email app, saving it as a file, or cancelling. Nothing is shared unless
you pick a destination yourself, and the app has no visibility into what
you choose.

When you use **Import Shift Data**, you pick a file with the system file
picker. The app reads only the file you select.

## Contacting the developer

When you tap **Contact Developer** in Settings, the app opens a new email
in your own email app, addressed to the developer. Below the space for
your message it adds one line of details — the app's version, your
phone's model, and its iOS or Android version — because bug reports
usually turn on them. You see everything before it's sent and can edit
or delete any of it. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, from
your email app; Tip Breakdown itself still makes no network requests.

If you do send it, the developer receives your message, those details,
and the email address you send from, and uses them only to reply to you.

## Children

Tip Breakdown is a workplace tool intended for adults. It does not
knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
